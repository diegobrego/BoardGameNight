How it works

Back to the site

Board Game Night helps our group find a day that works, pick what to play, and keep track of who won. Here's how to do the basics. Each part takes a minute.

Want to try things out first? Add ?demo to the end of the address. You get a private practice copy that nobody else sees and that never touches the real calendar.

Get started

The first time you open the site, it asks who you are. Nobody needs a password.

  1. Tap I'm new here.
  2. Type your name and pick a pixel face. More faces shows other ones.
  3. Tap Join. Your name and face now sit in the top right corner.

Already in the group? Tap your own name in the list instead. Please don't tap someone else's: it would let you change their days.

Join a game night

Tell the group which days you can play. When enough of us are in, the day lights up.

  1. On the Calendar page, tap Pick my days.
  2. Tap every day you can play. Those days turn purple. Tap a day again to un-pick it.
  3. Tap Save at the bottom.

Prefer one day at a time? Tap the day and then I'm available this day. Changed your mind later? Open the day and tap I can't make it.

A day with 3 or more players gets a green border and a Game on label: that's a game night. On the day before, and on the day itself, an orange banner at the top reminds everyone.

Once you're in for a day, you can also vote for the games you'd like to play there (the arrow) and tap I'll bring it on a game you own.

Add games to your collection and mark favourites

Your collection is the list of games you own or love. Fill it once and you never have to search for them again.

  1. Tap your name in the top right corner. Your profile opens.
  2. Tap Manage my collection.
  3. Type a game's name in the search box and tap the game you mean. It's added to My games. You can also paste a BoardGameGeek link.
  4. Tap the star next to a game to make it a favourite. Tap it again to take the star off. Favourites are listed first.
  5. Made a mistake? Tap the next to a game to remove it from your collection.

Everyone can see your collection on your player card (tap your name anywhere on the site), so the group knows who owns what.

Add a game to a day

Suggest what to play. As soon as one person is available on a day, games can be added to it, so the others can see the idea and join in.

  1. Tap the day on the calendar. A panel opens with who's available.
  2. Under Game options, tap Add a game.
  3. From your collection: tap Add game from collection, then tap the game. It goes on the day straight away. Games that are already there are greyed out.
  4. Something new: type the name in the search box and tap the game. You can also paste a BoardGameGeek link.

You need to be available on that day to vote for a game or to say you'll bring it. The game with the most votes gets a star badge (the top pick). A button on each game shows who is bringing it: tap it to say you will too.

Start or join a campaign

A campaign is a long game you play over several evenings, like Arcs or Oath. The site keeps track of the sessions and of when you play next.

Start one

  1. Open the Campaigns page and tap Start a campaign.
  2. Search for the game and tap it. Give the campaign a name if you like.
  3. Tick who's playing and tap Start. You're the creator.
  4. Others can now Join on their own. When everyone's in, tap Lock, everyone's in. After that only you can add people (with Add players).

Join one

  1. Open the Campaigns page and find the campaign.
  2. Tap Join. (If it says Locked, ask its creator to add you.)

Play it

  1. After a session, anyone in the campaign taps Log session: tick who played, say who won, add a note if you like.
  2. While you're all together, choose the Next session day in the same window. It shows on the calendar with a round flag, and everyone in the campaign gets a banner on the day before and the day itself.
  3. Finished? The creator taps Finish and picks the winner. Changed your mind? Reopen brings it back.

After you've played

  1. Open today on the calendar and tap Log game night.
  2. Pick the game, tick who played and choose the winner.
  3. It's saved in the Hall of fame, where you can see who wins and who plays the most. Tap any name to see their player card.

Forgot to log it on the day? Open Last week on the calendar and tap the day.

Good to know

  • Players tab: everyone in the group, with their wins, favourite games and titles. Tap a row for their full card.
  • The orange ! next to a game means nobody has it in their collection yet. If you own it, add it to yours (profile, then Manage my collection) and it disappears.
  • Campaign notes: every campaign has a Notes section that everyone in it can edit: where you left off, house rules, who plays which side.
  • Something wrong, or an idea? Tap Send feedback at the bottom of the page. Only the admin can read it.
  • Dark mode: the button at the top switches between light and dark.
  • Install it as an app: tap Install as an app at the bottom of the main page. It then opens like any other app.
  • Looks out of date? Reload with Ctrl + Shift + R. If a bar says A new version of the site is ready, tap Reload.
  • Wrong name or face? Tap your name, then Save after changing it. If you ended up as the wrong person, tap Switch player and pick yourself (or I'm new here).
